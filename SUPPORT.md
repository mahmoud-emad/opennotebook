# Getting help

- **How do I…? / ideas / show and tell:** [Discussions](https://github.com/mahmoud-emad/opennotebook/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** [open an issue](https://github.com/mahmoud-emad/opennotebook/issues/new/choose). Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened, and include the version and which AI provider you use.
- **A security problem:** please don't post it publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Setup problems are often a missing `POSTGRES_PASSWORD` or an AI key without credit. The setup tour in the app tests your key and says what is wrong.

If OpenNotebook is useful to you or your team, you can [sponsor its development](https://github.com/sponsors/mahmoud-emad).
